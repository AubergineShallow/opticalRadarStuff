FILE: geo.py
PURPOSE: Convert between different ways of describing locations on Earth.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS FILE DOES (Plain English)
================================================================================

This file converts between different ways of saying "where is this?"

Think about giving directions:
- "37.7749 North, 122.4194 West" (GPS coordinates)
- "50 meters East and 30 meters North of the park" (local directions)

Computers need to switch between these formats.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
LOCATION FORMATS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. GPS (Latitude, Longitude, Altitude)
   - What: The standard format from satellites
   - Example: San Francisco is at 37.7749°N, 122.4194°W
   - Problem: Hard to calculate distances

2. ECEF (Earth-Centered, Earth-Fixed)
   - What: X, Y, Z from Earth's center
   - Like if Earth was a ball and you measured from the middle
   - Good for: Precise calculations

3. ENU (East, North, Up)
   - What: Meters from a reference point
   - Example: "50m East, -20m North, 100m Up from home base"
   - Good for: Local calculations, easy to understand

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
FUNCTIONS (What you can do)
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. GPS TO ECEF
   - Take: latitude, longitude, altitude
   - Give: X, Y, Z from Earth's center
   - Uses: Earth's shape (not a perfect sphere)

2. ECEF TO ENU
   - Take: X, Y, Z position and a reference point
   - Give: meters East, North, Up from reference
   - Like: "How far am I from home?"

3. GPS TO LOCAL METERS
   - Take: GPS coordinates and a home base GPS
   - Give: meters East, North, Up from home base
   - Shortcut: Does both conversions at once

4. CALCULATE LOOK ANGLES
   - Take: Where you are, where the target is (both in ENU)
   - Give: Direction to look (compass heading and up/down angle)
   - Like: "To see the bird, look 45° to the right and 30° up"

================================================================================
WHY THIS MATTERS
================================================================================

- Cameras have GPS but need local coordinates
- The server needs to combine views from cameras in different places
- Distances like "how far is that object?" need local coordinates
