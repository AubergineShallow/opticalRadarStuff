FILE: ray_builder.py
PURPOSE: Convert camera reports into 3D lines pointing into the sky.

================================================================================
WHAT THIS FILE DOES (Plain English)
================================================================================

When a camera says "I see something at 45° right and 30° up", this file
converts that into a line that starts at the camera and goes into the sky.

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
THE PROCESS
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

INPUT (from camera):
  - Camera GPS location (latitude, longitude, altitude)
  - Camera rotation (which way it's pointing)
  - List of things it saw (direction angles)

OUTPUT:
  - List of 3D lines, each with:
    - Starting point (where the camera is)
    - Direction (which way the line goes)

--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
STEP BY STEP
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------

1. FIND CAMERA POSITION
   - Take GPS coordinates
   - Convert to local meters (East, North, Up from home base)
   - Now we know where the camera is in 3D space

2. GET CAMERA ROTATION
   - Take the rotation numbers from the camera
   - Convert to a rotation grid (3x3 matrix)
   - This tells us how to transform directions

3. FOR EACH THING THE CAMERA SAW:
   
   a. CONVERT ANGLES TO DIRECTION
      - Take the left/right angle and up/down angle
      - Calculate a unit direction (like an arrow pointing that way)
      - This is in "camera space" (relative to camera)
   
   b. TRANSFORM TO WORLD SPACE
      - Apply the camera rotation
      - Now the direction is in "world space" (relative to ground)
   
   c. CREATE A RAY
      - Start point = camera position
      - Direction = transformed direction
      
      * NEW: Apply Calibration Offset *
      - If this camera has a calibration correction (from Calibrator)
      - Rotate the direction by the correction quaternion
      
      - Add to the list

4. RETURN ALL RAYS
   - These rays can now be fed into the voxel grid

================================================================================
WHY THIS MATTERS
================================================================================

- Camera angles alone aren't useful (need 3D lines)
- The voxel grid needs lines to find where objects are
- Proper transformation accounts for camera tilt/rotation
